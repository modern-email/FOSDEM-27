# Modern Email DevRoom at FOSDEM 2027

Email, the probably most ubiquitous open and decentralized technology is about
to make an astonishing comeback. Built on aging technology stacks and suffering
from abuse as well as fierce competition by social networks, enterprise chat
and instant messaging, it has been called "dead" various times. For many years,
there had been no major novel projects and no dedicated email DevRoom at FOSDEM.

This has changed in recent years. On the technical side, new servers, clients
and software libraries are emerging - especially in novel programming languages
such as Flutter, Go, and Rust. Standards-wise, a long term effort of creating
the JMAP family of protocols is beginning to show effects.

Email is still the workhorse of digital communication and consumers as well
as enterprise users are increasingly questioning their dependency on
cloud-based solutions. Government initiatives for digital sovereignty are
pushing for Open Source solutions.

In this process of "modernizing email", protocols need to be updated,
specifications and implementations written, and tested for compatibility.
The Modern Email DevRoom, provides a platform for open source/standards
developers/experts/enthusiasts, sharing code, knowledge and getting to know
people in the community.

## Call for Participation

We'll have a "**Modern Email**" devroom again at FOSDEM 2027, and we're looking
for speakers!

We want to get together as email developers/users, learn about each other's
projects, have technical discussions, exchange implementation/operational
experiences, and share plans and visions for the future of email.

FOSDEM is an annual conference about free and open source software. It takes
place at the ULB Solbosch Campus, Brussels, Belgium on 30/31 Jan 2027. See
https://fosdem.org/2027/.

The devroom will be **held on Saturday 30 January 2027** (full day).
The **submission deadline for talks is Saturday, 21 November, 2026** (end of
day).

**Please distribute this CfP as widely as possible to interested parties!**

## Topics

Some suggestions for talks:

- Self-hosting email, to stay in control of your communication (data sovereignty),
  and to keep email decentralized. What are the experiences, bottlenecks, and how
  can we make it easier?
- Your free/open source software project(s) related to email. From demo to details.
- Current developments, upcoming standards, e.g. [JMAP](https://jmap.io),
  [structured email](https://structured.email), ...
- Email and AI, Agentic Email
- Efforts to modernize our email ecosystem, e.g., through implementations in
  modern languages
- Integrating email with other software, e.g., through APIs
- Message/transport security/authenticity
- Experiences with deliverability, email operations, anti-spam, hosting, ...
- Email in the wider sense of groupware solutions involving address books, 
  calendaring, text and video collaboration, and files
- Visionary talks

## Submission

We ask for a short talk abstract (+ proposed duration) and a speaker biography
(+ contact info). Any material that shows your affinity to the field is also
appreciated, e.g., a collection of links to your work, talks, projects, blog
posts, etc.

Submissions must be made through Pretalx (https://fosdem.org/submit) no later
than **Saturday, 21 November, 2026**. Be sure to propose a talk at
[pretalx.fosdem.org](https://pretalx.fosdem.org/fosdem-2027/cfp) and
select the "Modern email Track".

The suggested duration for a talk is **25 minutes** (20 minutes presentation +
5 minutes Q&A). However, we *explicitly* invite you to propose the best
duration for your talk. If the best way to present your project is a **5-minute
lightning talk**, we'll happily arrange a lightning session!

Depending on the proposals, we may contact you to adapt the time of the session
to fit the DevRoom schedule.

Once the DevRoom schedule is finalized, we will contact you to help you prepare
your talk.
